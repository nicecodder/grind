Health & Fitness Data: We collect data you voluntarily log within the app, including workout sessions, study hours, sleep patterns, water intake, and step counts. This data is stored locally on your device and synced to our servers only when you are signed in.

Usage Data: We automatically collect anonymous usage analytics such as app interactions, feature usage patterns, session duration, and crash reports to improve our services.

Device Information: We may collect device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers for functionality and security purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To personalize your experience, including habit recommendations and achievement tracking

  • To process subscriptions and manage premium features (Grind Pro & Elite)
  • To send push notifications (only with your explicit consent)
  • To analyze aggregate, anonymized usage trends to improve our services
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues or abuse

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • Encryption: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.3
  • Secure Storage: Personal data is stored in encrypted databases with access controls
  • Authentication: We use OAuth 2.0 for secure Google Sign-In integration
  • Regular Audits: We conduct periodic security audits and vulnerability assessments

While we strive to protect your information, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share limited data with:

  • Service Providers: Trusted third-party services that help us operate the app (e.g., cloud hosting, analytics, payment processing)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or governmental authority
  • Safety: To protect the rights, property, or safety of Grind, our users, or the public

All third-party service providers are contractually obligated to maintain the confidentiality and security of your data.

5. Your Rights & Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data at any time
  • Correction: Update or correct any inaccurate information in your profile
  • Deletion: Request the deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Opt-Out: Disable notifications and analytics collection at any time through the app settings
  • Data Portability: Export your fitness and habit data in a standard format

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@grindapp.com.

6. Children's Privacy

Grind is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will promptly delete it.
