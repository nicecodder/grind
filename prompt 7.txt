ou are building the subscription/paywall screen for an iOS app called Grind — a daily life EXP tracker where users complete real-life tasks (fitness, study, work, habits) to earn experience points, level up, and compete on a global leaderboard.
Design language: Clean, minimal, dark. Inspired by Spotify and YouTube Premium paywalls. No gradients, no glow, no blur, no shadows, no neon. Flat dark cards only. Everything should feel native iOS but premium.

SCREEN LAYOUT (top to bottom):

Status bar (system default)
Navigation bar — back chevron left, centered title "Upgrade", no right button
Hero section — headline "Pick your grind level", subtitle "Unlock faster EXP, protect your streak, and climb the leaderboard."
Billing toggle — pill-shaped segmented control with two options: "Monthly" and "Annual (Save 33%)". Annual option has a small green pill badge saying "Save 33%". Toggle switches all prices on the screen instantly.
Three plan cards stacked vertically with 10pt gap
Primary CTA button
Secondary ghost button
Footer legal text

When user taps "Monthly": show $2.99/mo for Pro, $4.99/mo for Elite
When user taps "Annual": show $1.99/mo (billed $23.88/yr) for Pro, $3.33/mo (billed $39.99/yr) for Elite
The CTA button price text also updates to match the selected billing cycle.
Animate the price change with a simple 0.2s fade or crossDissolve transition.
